## I1 disposition

1. **Owner is independent of content.** Spec lines 31 and 39 introduce internal `IndexRootOwner={canonicalRoot,identity}`. Identity must reliably distinguish the actual directory on its platform/filesystem. Original rootPath, including an alias, is re-realpathed for each observation. Path/bytes/mtime/size/watch filename cannot stand in for ownership. Missing, unreliable or unreadable identity is unavailable and cannot compare equal via an empty or zero placeholder. The document accurately says existing scan presently returns a canonical string, not this owner witness.
2. **Admitted ownership survives captures and fallback.** Spec line 41 establishes the owner only through explicit path-confirmed open in the existing project queue, fixes owner and epoch at admission, and requires capture-start/end checks for both complete captures against that same owner. Candidate content and owner witnesses remain distinct. Canonical-path and byte equality do not excuse different directory identity. Existing-owner full, approve freshness and all cache/fallback modes retain the owner; they do not silently admit a replacement.
3. **Publication has a real lock-held fence.** Spec line 43 and plan Task 4 require another observation from the original path in `StructureSync.withProject`, comparing current owner, both candidate owners, expected owner and session/global epoch. After observation resolves, execution immediately hands off to the synchronous joint transaction without an intervening await, queue or further analysis. Mismatch/unavailable discards candidate/cache, invalidates epoch and closes watcher/timers/hints before storeSnapshot, knowledge reconciliation or plan invalidation. Existing pointer, revisions, history and approval context remain unchanged. This fits the existing queue and synchronous SQL publication seam; actual source must still implement it.
4. **Recovery cannot rebind silently.** Ordinary full refresh, retry, background open/reconciliation and force-full cannot recreate or re-admit the failed owner/session. Only explicit path-confirmed reopen can establish a new owner. It does not migrate knowledge bindings or clear the independent user-stop disable flag. A compatibility adapter without the owner capability may retain its old manual-index contract, but cannot enable automatic listening or advertise owner protection. Advertising observeRoot requires enforcing expectedRoot inside index, rather than merely supplying an optional method.
5. **The contract has executable future negative gates.** Spec acceptance item 4 and plan Tasks 2/4 require real owned directory replacement with identical source/config/inventory bytes during analysis and after the second capture but before publication; alias retargeting; suppressed/delayed watcher hints; and unavailable identity. Assertions cover actual root observation, root_changed/stopped, unchanged pointer/knowledge/history/revisions/invalidation count, no candidate/cache/session revival, retry/full/reconciliation rejection and explicit reopen. Platform identity capability remains a real native acceptance gate; mocked IDs do not satisfy it.
6. **The external filesystem boundary is honest.** Spec line 45 expressly promises fail-closed behavior for observed mismatch and does not claim an atomic filesystem/SQLite snapshot or exclusion of an unobserved external change after the final owner observation. This addresses I1 without inventing a cross-filesystem locking guarantee. Actual observed mismatch remains a hard rejection rather than merely another refresh hint.
